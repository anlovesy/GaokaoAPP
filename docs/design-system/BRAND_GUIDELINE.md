# BRAND_GUIDELINE

## Final Identity Direction

The selected brand mark direction for this project is:

- `Decision Orbit`

This mark should represent:

- the user as the center of the decision
- AI as the orbit of reasoning around that center
- the final arrow as calibrated direction

Supporting references:

- [ZHIXU_LOGO_SYSTEM_DECISION_ORBIT_v1.md](../ZHIXU_LOGO_SYSTEM_DECISION_ORBIT_v1.md)
- [ZHIXU_BRAND_GUIDELINE_v1.md](../ZHIXU_BRAND_GUIDELINE_v1.md)

## Brand Keywords

- Professional
- Premium
- Intelligent
- Calm
- Strategic
- Trustworthy
- Future-facing
- Minimal

## Brand Temperament

This brand should feel like:

- a private strategic consultant
- an elite decision partner
- a modern AI product with restraint

This brand should not feel like:

- a school portal
- a government site
- a parent forum
- a feature-heavy dashboard

## Brand Positioning Statement

AI Gaokao Advisor is a premium strategic decision product for students and families facing one of the most important choices in their education journey.

Preferred brand naming:

- Chinese: `志序`
- English: `ZHIXU`
- Descriptor: `AI College Decision Studio`

It does not sell information.

It sells:

- clarity
- confidence
- strategy
- trustworthy judgment

## Emotional Goal

Users should feel:

- reassured
- focused
- respected
- guided
- more certain after each interaction

The product should reduce panic and replace it with clarity.

## Emotional Sequence

Across a full session, the emotional journey should move through:

1. curiosity
2. reassurance
3. concentration
4. confidence
5. decision readiness

The product should never push users toward:

- anxiety
- urgency theater
- visual pressure
- choice paralysis

## Visual Language

- Large editorial headlines
- Strong whitespace
- Soft depth instead of heavy framing
- Cinematic image usage
- Minimal chrome
- Glass only when it improves atmosphere
- Quiet surfaces with selective glow
- Orbit-based brand motion and directional cues

## Trust Signals

Trust should be communicated through:

- disciplined typography
- restrained motion
- accurate image usage
- clean interaction feedback
- clear data hierarchy

Trust should not depend on:

- excessive numbers
- loud visual badges
- promotional copy
- crowded screens

## Photography Direction

Photography must feel:

- architectural
- atmospheric
- bright but not cheerful
- real, not stock-banner style

Preferred subjects:

- campus gates
- libraries
- lecture halls
- corridors
- iconic buildings
- dusk and early morning campus light

Avoid:

- handshake photos
- smiling classroom stock photos
- certificates
- crowds with banners
- generic education posters

## Typography Strategy

- Headline type carries authority and premium character.
- Body type must remain highly legible in Chinese.
- Utility type may be used for data, timestamps, or subtle labels.

Approved hierarchy lives in:

- [TYPOGRAPHY.md](TYPOGRAPHY.md)

## Color Strategy

- The palette should feel quiet, intelligent, and premium.
- Deep teal and ink tones establish trust.
- Warm coral is reserved for emphasis and decision points.
- Bright accents must be rare.

Approved palette lives in:

- [COLOR_SYSTEM.md](COLOR_SYSTEM.md)

## Whitespace Strategy

- Whitespace is a primary design tool, not leftover space.
- Every screen should breathe.
- Density should feel curated, never crowded.

Approved spacing rules live in:

- [SPACING_SYSTEM.md](SPACING_SYSTEM.md)

## Motion Strategy

- Motion must feel slow, confident, and spatial.
- The system should feel alive, not busy.
- Ambient motion is allowed.
- Distracting animation is not allowed.
- Decision Orbit is the preferred logo and loading motion base.

Approved motion rules live in:

- [MOTION_SPEC.md](MOTION_SPEC.md)

## Content Philosophy

- Do not explain the product to the customer.
- Do not overload screens with feature descriptions.
- Do not fill premium space with defensive text.

Each screen should prioritize:

- one thesis
- one supporting line
- one action

## Copy Voice

The voice should be:

- calm
- direct
- respectful
- modern
- never overpromising

The voice should avoid:

- slogans that sound like ads
- feature descriptions
- defensive explanations
- overly academic wording
- casual humor that weakens trust

## Language Rules

- Prefer short statements over long paragraphs.
- Prefer product confidence over explanatory text.
- Prefer action labels that describe outcomes.
- Use sentence case.
- Keep support copy concise and functional.

## Interaction Philosophy

- The interface should feel composed before it feels interactive.
- Every interaction must confirm intent.
- Feedback should be immediate, refined, and quiet.

## Anti-Patterns

The brand fails if the interface relies on:

- dashboard cards everywhere
- equal-weight modules competing for attention
- colorful KPI surfaces
- school brochure photography
- long feature lists
- tutorial blocks on customer-facing screens
- heavy borders replacing hierarchy

## Visual Quality Bar

The final product should feel closer to:

- a premium Apple-style product page
- a modern AI-native work environment
- a high-end digital consulting brand

It should feel further from:

- internal tooling
- admin portals
- local education portals
- sales-heavy SaaS landing pages

## Final Standard

The brand should feel like a high-value AI advisory service with international product quality.

If a screen feels like a dashboard, the design has failed.
