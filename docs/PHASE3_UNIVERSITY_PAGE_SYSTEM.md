# Phase 3 University Page System

## Objective

Turn the university page into a premium decision review surface.

Its role is not to behave like a data portal or an admissions encyclopedia.
Its real job is:

`help the user judge whether one university deserves a place in the final sequence`

The page should feel like:

- a decision dossier
- a calm review room
- a premium editorial campus brief

It should not feel like:

- a school information platform
- a statistics dashboard
- a table-heavy admin page
- a long educational article

---

## Page Role

The University Page sits between:

- `Workspace` as the system-level decision studio
- `Volunteer` as the ordering and sequencing page
- `AI Advisor` as the active reasoning companion

This page answers one question:

`Should this university move upward, stay, or be replaced?`

---

## Core Layout

### Desktop Ratio

- Left Rail: `18%`
- Main Review Canvas: `58%`
- Right Decision Rail: `24%`

### Layout Principle

- Left: campus identity and quick facts
- Center: the main story of fit, probability, and major direction
- Right: AI judgment, compare actions, and sequence impact

### Spatial Behavior

- outer padding: `48 - 64`
- rail gap: `28 - 32`
- section gap: `32 - 40`
- micro spacing: `12 - 16`

The center must remain the visual focus.

---

## Core Sections

### 1. Campus Hero

Content:

- Eyebrow
- University name
- One-line positioning
- Large campus image
- Short decision status

Purpose:

- establish emotional identity first
- make the school feel real, not abstract data
- immediately answer whether this is a strong-fit, stretch-fit, or safety-fit option

Rules:

- the hero image should dominate the first read
- no dense fact wall in the hero
- title remains short and calm

### 2. Fit Overview

Content:

- Match Summary
- Major Fit
- City Fit
- Rank Pressure

Purpose:

- compress complex suitability into a readable editorial block

Rules:

- not four identical cards
- use asymmetric editorial modules
- one core sentence + three supporting judgments

### 3. Major Constellation

Content:

- target majors
- alternative majors
- acceptance confidence by direction
- course / discipline tone

Purpose:

- show the academic shape of the university

Rules:

- more like a constellation map than a checklist
- each major node should feel connected to the decision path

### 4. Admission Pressure Review

Content:

- recent score / rank pressure
- probability
- risk level
- volatility note

Purpose:

- translate historical risk into a calm judgment surface

Rules:

- not chart-heavy
- not spreadsheet-like
- use one central confidence expression and one concise warning

### 5. Sequence Impact Strip

Content:

- if moved upward
- if kept in current place
- if replaced

Purpose:

- explain how this one school affects the broader volunteer order

Rules:

- should feel like a strategic consequence strip
- short, directional, and comparative

### 6. AI Decision Rail

Content:

- Current Judgment
- Why it matters
- Compare with nearby options
- Move Up / Hold / Replace prompts

Purpose:

- make the page actionable without turning it into a chat UI

Rules:

- not a mobile phone frame
- not a message list
- more like a calm decision control rail

---

## Visual Language

The University Page inherits the shared ZHIXU language:

- Orbit
- Path
- Connection
- Decision

Mapping:

- hero image = context and identity
- major constellation = academic path network
- probability surface = decision pressure
- sequence strip = consequence line
- AI rail = orbital judgment layer

---

## Surface Rules

- Base canvas: warm white / fog gray
- Hero image surface: soft glass overlay
- Analysis surfaces: translucent white
- AI rail: deep navy glass

### Border

- hairline only
- low contrast

### Shadow

- almost invisible
- depth should come from blur, contrast, and image layering

### Radius

- hero surface: `34 - 40`
- content modules: `24 - 30`
- pills / status: `18 - 22`

---

## Typography

### Display

- 54 - 64px
- medium to semibold
- used only once in the hero

### Heading

- 28 - 34px

### Body

- 17 - 18px

### Caption

- 12 - 14px
- uppercase English labels allowed

Rules:

- avoid black-heavy Chinese headers
- hierarchy should come from size, spacing, and restraint

---

## Motion Intent

The final implementation should support:

- Hero image slow parallax
- fit nodes breathing hover
- probability pulse
- decision rail glow focus
- sequence strip reveal on scroll

Motion should feel deliberate, not decorative.

---

## Exit Criteria

This page is complete when:

1. it feels like a premium university decision review, not a school database
2. the center canvas clearly dominates the reading order
3. users can understand fit, risk, and sequence impact at a glance
4. it visually belongs to the same family as Workspace and Volunteer
